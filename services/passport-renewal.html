Passport renewal: when should you renew?

Many countries, and most airlines, won't let you travel if your passport has less than six months of validity left. In the UAE, an expiring passport can also affect your residence visa and Emirates ID. Renewing early saves a lot of trouble.

  • Your passport expires within the next 6–12 months
  • You're running out of blank visa pages
  • Your passport is damaged, worn or water-stained
  • Your name or personal details have changed
  • Your passport has been lost or stolen

Typical documents for passport renewal

  • Your current (or expired) passport and a copy of the bio page
  • Recent passport photos to your country's specification
  • Copy of your UAE residence visa and Emirates ID (for residents)
  • National ID card or birth certificate, if your embassy requires it
  • Police report, for lost or stolen passports

Degree attestation needs your original degree certificate and transcripts. For the certificates, bring your passport or any ID you have, and your UAE visa or Emirates ID.

The process

  1. Bring your documents. Visit our office or send us the details to start.
  2. We prepare the file. Forms, photos and copies, all checked before your appointment.
  3. Submission. You attend the Embassy (if required) with a ready file, or we submit where allowed.
  4. Collection & update. Collect your passport or certificate, and we'll help update your UAE records if needed.
