Documents you'll usually need

Requirements depend on the country and your nationality. For most visit visas, start with:

  • A clear colour copy of your passport, valid for at least 6 months
  • A recent passport-size photo on a white background
  • A return flight ticket and hotel booking (we can arrange both for you)
  • A copy of your UAE residence visa or Emirates ID, if you're a UAE resident travelling abroad
  • Some countries also ask for a recent bank statement

Not sure what applies to you? Send us a photo of your passport bio page on WhatsApp and we'll tell you exactly what's needed. There's no charge for the check.

How it works

  1. Talk to us. Visit the office, call or message us with your destination, travel dates and nationality.
  2. Document check. We review everything before submission, which is when most problems get caught.
  3. We apply and book. We submit your visa application and, if you like, book your flights, hotel, insurance and entry tickets.
  4. Ready to travel. We send your approved visa and bookings as soon as they're issued.

Why book with Mahar Adipati?

  • Fewer rejections: careful document checks before anything is submitted.
  • Transparent fees: visa fees and our service charge, itemised up front.
  • One desk for the whole trip: visa, flights, hotel, insurance and tickets in one place.

Processing times are set by each country's immigration authorities and can vary, so apply early, especially around holidays and peak travel seasons.
