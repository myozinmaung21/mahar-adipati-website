International Air Tickets

Flights from the UAE to anywhere in the world. Tell us where and when, and we'll find the fare and book it for you.

Mahar Adipati Travel Desk

Searching dozens of websites for the right flight takes time, and it's easy to miss a better fare or a baggage rule. Tell us where you're going and when, and we'll compare the options and book the one that suits you.

What we book

One-way & ReturnFlights from the UAE to destinations worldwide, or back home.
Multi-city TripsVisiting more than one country? We plan the route and book every leg.
Families & GroupsSeats for the whole family or group on the same booking.
Changes & RebookingHelp changing your travel dates when plans change, subject to the airline's fare rules.

Fares change daily. Send us your destination, travel dates and number of passengers, and we'll give you the current fare and baggage allowance before you book.

What we need from you

  • Each passenger's name exactly as it appears in their passport
  • A copy of each passport, valid for at least 6 months from your travel date
  • Your travel dates, and whether your dates are flexible
  • The visa for your destination, if one is needed (we can arrange that too)

Make it a complete trip

Book your flight together with your visit visa, hotel and travel insurance, and we'll make sure all your dates line up. Flying into the UAE? We can also arrange an airport pick-up.

Book in four steps

  1. Tell us your trip. Destination, dates and number of passengers.
  2. Choose your fare. We send you the best options we find, with prices and baggage allowance.
  3. Confirm and pay. Send us the passport copies and we'll issue your tickets.
  4. Fly. Your e-tickets arrive by WhatsApp or email, ready for check-in.
