Tour Packages

Desert safaris, dinner cruises, private yachts and sightseeing tours. Tell us what you'd like to do and we'll plan and book it for you.

Mahar Adipati Travel Desk

The UAE has a lot to offer beyond the city, from golden dunes to evenings on the water. Whether it's a family day out, a celebration or showing visiting relatives around, we'll find the right package and book it for you.

Our tour packages

Desert SafariDune bashing, camel rides and an evening at a desert camp with dinner.
Cruise DinnerAn evening on the water with dinner on board and city skyline views.
Yacht RentalPrivate yacht trips for family outings, birthdays and celebrations.
UAE Tour PackageSightseeing tours of the top landmarks in Abu Dhabi, Dubai and the other Emirates.

Prices depend on the date and group size. Contact us with the package you'd like, your date and the number of people, and we'll send you a quote.

Good to know

  • Book ahead: weekends, public holidays and the cooler winter months fill up fast.
  • Groups and families: tell us how many adults and children are coming so we can find the right option.
  • Celebrations: planning a birthday or special occasion on a yacht or cruise? Let us know when you book.
  • Visiting the UAE? We can arrange your visit visa, airport pick-up and tours together.

Book in four steps

  1. Choose your package. Tell us what you'd like to do, your date and the number of people.
  2. Get a quote. One clear price, so you know exactly what you're paying for.
  3. Confirm your booking. We reserve your date and send you the details.
  4. Enjoy your day. Everything is arranged, so all you have to do is turn up and enjoy it.
