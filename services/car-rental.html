Car Rental & Transfers

Airport pick-up and drop-off, and car rental with or without a driver, all booked from one desk in Abu Dhabi.

Mahar Adipati Travel Desk

Whether you're landing in the UAE for the first time or need a car for the month, we'll arrange it for you. Tell us what you need and we'll take care of the rest.

Our services

Airport Pick-up & Drop-offA driver meets you at arrivals and takes you to your hotel or home, and back again for your flight.
Car RentalSelf-drive or with a driver, by the day, week or month.

Prices depend on the date, car and trip. Contact us with what you'd like to book and your dates, and we'll send you a quote.

Car rental, your way

Self-DriveTake the wheel yourself and explore on your own schedule.
With a DriverSit back while a professional driver takes you to meetings, events or sightseeing.

Choose your car

  • Economy: compact and fuel-efficient, ideal for city driving
  • Sedan: more room and comfort for business trips and longer drives
  • SUV: space for the whole family and luggage
  • Premium: for special occasions, or when you want something a little nicer

Flexible rental plans

  • Daily: for short trips and weekend getaways
  • Weekly: better rates for holidays and visiting family
  • Monthly: the best value for residents and long stays

What you'll need for self-drive

  • UAE residents: valid UAE driving licence and Emirates ID
  • Visitors: passport with visit visa or entry stamp, your home driving licence, and an International Driving Permit where required
  • A credit or debit card for the refundable security deposit

Good to know: for self-drive rentals, Salik (road toll) charges and traffic fines incurred during the rental are billed back after the rental ends. We explain the fuel policy and insurance cover before you sign.

Book in four steps

  1. Tell us what you need. Airport transfer or car rental, your dates and the number of passengers.
  2. Get a quote. One clear price, with any deposit shown separately.
  3. Confirm your booking. Send the documents we need and we'll lock in your date.
  4. On your way. Your driver or car is ready on the day.
