About Us

Travel made easier, one client at a time

Mahar Adipati Travel Company is an Abu Dhabi travel and documentation office. We help residents, visitors and families with everything a trip involves: international air tickets, visas, passport renewals, a reliable car once you arrive, and tours and experiences to enjoy while you're here.

Every application is reviewed by a real person before it's submitted. We explain what's needed, quote our fees up front and keep you updated until the job is finished. Our clients come back, and send their friends, because we treat their plans as if they were our own.

  • Clear pricingKnow the full cost before you commit.
  • Fast follow-upWe track every file until it's done.
